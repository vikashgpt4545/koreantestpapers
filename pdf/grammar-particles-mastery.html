Core Particles Breakdown

Detailed Rules, Distinctions & Workplace Examples
1. Topic Marking Particle (-은 / -는) Topic & Contrast

Used to state the main topic of a sentence or to make a contrast between two elements ("As for...", "Regarding...").

Grammar Rule:
• Attach -은 to nouns ending in a Consonants (받침): 공장 ➔ 공장은, 작업 ➔ 작업은
• Attach -는 to nouns ending in a Vowel (no 받침): 반장 ➔ 반장은, 기계 ➔ 기계는
Korean Workplace Sentence English Translation Exam Key Point
이 기계는 아주 안전합니다. As for this machine, it is very safe. Topic marker after vowel '기계'
오늘 작업은 6시에 끝납니다. Today's work finishes at 6 o'clock. Topic marker after consonant '작업'
저는 용접은 잘하지만 절단은 못합니다. I am good at welding, but I cannot do cutting. Contrast between welding and cutting
2. Subject Marking Particle (-이 / -가) Subject & New Information

Identifies the subject of an action or state. Used when introducing new information or emphasizing the subject.

Grammar Rule:
• Attach -이 to nouns ending in a Consonant (받침): 부품 ➔ 부품이, 안전모 ➔ 안전모가
• Attach -가 to nouns ending in a Vowel (no 받침): 관리자 ➔ 관리자가, 전선 ➔ 전선이
Korean Workplace Sentence English Translation Exam Key Point
안전모가 어디에 있습니까? Where is the safety helmet? Subject focus on '안전모'
새 부품이 도착했습니다. The new spare parts have arrived. New information announcement
누구가 스위치를 켰습니까? Who turned on the switch? Emphasis on question word '누구'
3. Object Marking Particle (-을 / -를) Direct Object

Attaches to a noun that receives the direct action of a transitive verb (e.g. operate, fix, clean, wear).

Grammar Rule:
• Attach -을 to nouns ending in a Consonant (받침): 장갑 ➔ 장갑을, 기계 ➔ 기계를
• Attach -를 to nouns ending in a Vowel (no 받침): 차 ➔ 차를, 자재 ➔ 자재를
Korean Workplace Sentence English Translation Exam Key Point
작업 전에 안전화를 착용하세요. Please wear safety boots before work. Object of action verb '착용하다'
볼트를 렌치로 조입니다. Tighten the bolt with a wrench. Object of action verb '조이다'
폐기물을 분리해서 버리세요. Separate and throw away the industrial waste. Object of action verb '버리다'
4. Dynamic Location & Origin Particle (-에서) Action Location / From

1) Indicates the location where an action takes place ("At / In").
2) Indicates the starting point or origin ("From").

Grammar Rule: Attach -에서 to location nouns regardless of consonant/vowel ending.
Korean Workplace Sentence English Translation Exam Key Point
작업장에서 담배를 피우지 마세요. Do not smoke in the workplace. Location where smoking action occurs
창고에서 자재를 가져왔습니다. I brought the materials from the warehouse. Starting origin point ('From')
식당에서 점심을 먹습니다. We eat lunch in the cafeteria. Location of eating action
5. Time, Destination & Existence Particle (-에) Time / Destination / Location

Used for specific time, target destination of movement (가다/오다), or location of existence (있다/없다).

Korean Workplace Sentence English Translation Exam Key Point
8시에 조회가 시작됩니다. The morning meeting starts at 8 o'clock. Specific time marker
내일 사무실에 갑니다. I will go to the office tomorrow. Destination target of '가다'
공구함에 망치가 있습니다. There is a hammer in the toolbox. Static location of existence ('있다')
6. Range, Connective & Instrument Particles Advanced Particles
Particle Workplace Example Sentence Meaning
-과 / -와 반장님과 대리님이 회의 중입니다. And / With (Formal)
-하고 볼트하고 너트를 준비하세요. And / With (Conversational)
-부터 ~ -까지 9시부터 6시까지 근무합니다. From ~ Until (Time / Range)
-로 / -으로 지게차로 상자를 운반하세요. By means of / Tool used
-에게 / -한테 관리자에게 보고했습니다. To someone (Receiver)

EPS-TOPIK Particle Practice Drills

Test Your Knowledge with Official Pattern Questions

Fill in the blank with the correct particle:

  1. 작업장 (      ) 안전모를 꼭 써야 합니다.    [Choice: ① 에   ② 에서   ③ 을   ④ 은]
  2. 망치 (      ) 못을 박으세요.    [Choice: ① 로   ② 에게   ③ 과   ④ 가]
  3. 오늘 (      ) 야간 작업을 하지 않습니다.    [Choice: ① 는   ② 를   ③ 에서   ④ 과]
  4. 스패너 (      ) 어딨습니까?    [Choice: ① 가   ② 를   ③ 로   ④ 에서]
  5. 오전 8시 (      ) 오후 5시까지 일합니다.    [Choice: ① 부터   ② 까지   ③ 에서   ④ 과]
Answer Key & Explanations:
1. ② 에서 (Location of wearing safety helmet action)
2. ① 로 (Instrument tool 'by means of hammer')
3. ① 는 (Topic/contrast marker for 'Today')
4. ① 가 (Subject marker asking location of spanner)
5. ① 부터 (Starting time point 'From 8 AM')
